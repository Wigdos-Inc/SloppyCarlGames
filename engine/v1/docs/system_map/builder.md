# builder/ — Scene and Asset Construction

## Responsibility
Constructs all visual and structural scene data: 3D WebGL meshes, entity models, obstacles, terrain, null-space void walls, procedural textures, scatter details, UI element trees, and the full level scene graph. Builders are called by handlers, produce output objects, and return. They hold no persistent state and drive no update loops.

## Files
- `NewLevel.js` — Level scene graph assembler. `BuildLevel` is the primary entry point. Orchestrates all other builders to produce the complete `sceneGraph` consumed by the renderer and physics pipeline. Also exports `RefreshDebugBoundingBoxes`. It owns the debug-bounds collection: `buildSceneBoundingBoxes` and `buildSceneDetailedBounds` place voids in their own `Void` bucket, and `pushVoid` emits a void's volume, its void walls and its open faces as separately *coloured* records (`Void`/`VoidWall`/`VoidOpenFace`) sharing the one `Void` *toggle*. It resolves scatter density (`PERFORMANCE_SCALING.Density.Scatter[CONFIG.Performance.Scatter.Density]`) once and passes it to `BuildScatterBatches` as `scatterMultiplier`.

  **Build order.** Terrain and obstacles → `BuildVoidWalls` → `CarveOccluders(terrain, voidTerrain)` (see `NewTerrain.js`) → surface chunks → `sceneGraph.tubeCenterlines` (a `Map` surface id → centerline lines, gathered from terrain, void terrain and obstacle parts' `mesh.centerline`) → scatter batches → triggers → entities → level-authored particle generators. The definition surface map (`buildSurfaceMap(terrainDefinitions, obstacleDefinitions)`: `{ position, dimensions, scale, topY }` per terrain and obstacle id, voids included) is built **once** and published as `sceneGraph.spawnSurfaces`; the level's entities and the player (`player/Master.js`'s `InitializePlayer`) both spawn against it. `buildTriggerMesh` centres a trigger box at `start.y + triggerHeight × 0.5`, so the drawn and detected box spans `start.y` to `world.height`.

  **Surface chunks and loop surfaces.** `buildSurfaceChunks(terrain, obstacles, voids)` writes `sceneGraph.chunks` (`[{ id, surfaceIds }]`) and `sceneGraph.loopSurfaces` (`{ Air: Map, Water: Map }`, surface id → loop radius). Its pieces are the surfaces the ground probe grounds on: terrain and obstacle `detailedBounds` facets via `boundsFacets` (`[]` for `null` bounds), with void-suppressed facets dropped (`physics/Collision.js`'s `IsPointInSuppressingVoid` at each facet centroid), plus each void's lining soups under the void's own id. `boundsFacets` turns AABB bounds into an obb through `ObbFromAabb`. Facets link by shared edges within a piece and, across pieces whose bounds touch, by `TriangleDistanceSq` ≤ `EPSILON²`. Each link records whether it is concave and its fold angle.
  - **Chunks** are pieces joined by any link.
  - **Loop surfaces** are built per medium. Take the components joined by concave folds within the medium's `MaxAngleDelta.Ground` that contain both a floor (≤ `Ground`) and a ceiling (≥ `CAMERA_TUNING.Loops.MinCurveThreshold`). Within those, the steeper-than-ground sub-components that reach the ceiling are the loops.
  - **Loop radius.** Each loop takes its median `foldRadius` (facet width across the fold over `4·tan(fold/2)`), or `Infinity` with no folds. A surface in several loops keeps the smallest.

  `camera/Master.js` reads `loopSurfaces` and `tubeCenterlines` for loop tracking and the small-loop roll exception (see camera.md).
- `NewPrimitive.js` — Primitive shape construction. Its only export is `BuildGeometry(shape, size, complexity, primitiveOptions)`, which `NewObject.js` and `NewScatter.js` call; it imports `TransformPointByMatrix` from `math/Matrix.js`. It holds the complexity resolvers (`resolveCylinderSegments` 8/12/16, `resolveSphereResolution` with 7/13/19 stacks, `resolveCapsuleCapStacks`), the ring/fan/sector helpers and every `build<Shape>` function.
  - **Orientation.** Every builder emits **outward**-wound triangles. `buildPyramid` is centred on its origin (base at `−y/2`, apex at `+y/2`). `appendRadialVertices` takes an `angleOffset`: cylinders, capsules and every tube ring turn by `π / segments`, and spheres add `π / slices` to `theta`, so curved primitives lie flat when rotated 90°. Tube connector face groups add the same offset to each sector's `midAngle`. Cone and torus are unchanged.
  - **Tube.** A **node-chain bone walk**: `resolveTubeNodes` starts at an identity root frame and composes each `primitiveOptions.nodes[]` entry onto the running frame, so a node's `localPosition` is applied in the previous node's rotated axes. `makeTubeNode` extracts each node's `center`, `forward` (frame Y), `xAxis` and `zAxis`. `buildTube` lays one ring at every node (`appendTubeRing`), then for each consecutive pair: if `nodeA.curved` and the centers are more than `EPSILON` apart, it samples a connector centerline (`SampleConnectorCenterline`) and lofts intermediate rings along `ParallelTransportFrames`, interpolating radii and thickness; `orientConnectorFrames` distributes the residual roll so the join is seamless. Rings are stitched pairwise (`stitchTubeRings`), the ends capped (`capTubeRing`), and per-connector face groups emitted as angular sectors with radial normals (`appendTubeConnectorFaceGroups`). `primitiveOptions.solid` switches the chain to a swept solid (outer rings only, disc caps). It returns the tube's centerline (node centers plus connector ring points) as `geometry.centerline`.
  - **Ramps carry no angle.** `resolveRampShape(size)` derives the incline from `dimensions.y` and `.z` and returns `rise`, the corner coordinates and the `slopeNormal`. `ramp-simple` takes no options; `ramp-complex` takes a unitless 0–1 `depth` that curves its top along a quadratic Bézier from the straight chord (`0`) to tangent-at-toe and vertical-at-crest (`1`).
  - The decal surface map in `handlers/Render.js` (`DecalSurfaceUv`) assumes only the analytic shape of `buildSphere`/`buildCylinder`/`buildCapsule` — a Y-axis cylinder, and a capsule whose cap height is `primitiveOptions.rounding × y/2` with horizontal radii `x/2`/`z/2` — which must stay in sync if either changes.
- `NewObject.js` — Mesh builder over `NewPrimitive.js`'s geometry: UVs, bounds, world boxes and the mesh literal. Exports `BuildObject`, `UpdateObjectWorldAabb`, `ComputeWorldAabbFromBounds`, `GenerateUVs` and `GenerateFaceProjectedUvs`. `BuildObject` declares one base `mesh` (id, type, meta, transform, dimensions, collisionShape, worldAabb, detailedBounds, customTextures, `performance: { rendering: true }`) that each mode extends, and every return is `{ mesh }`. Void and invisible meshes carry the `performance` block too. Every terrain/obstacle mesh carries `centerline`: a tube's centerline in world space (`computeWorldCenterline`), `null` otherwise.
  - **Entity parts** (role `"entity-part"`) build through `buildEntityPartGeometryTemplate`, which constructs a `DeepFreeze`d `{ positions, indices, uvs, bounds, faceTextureGroups?, triplanar? }` template; with a caller-supplied `geometryCache` it is memoized per `blueprintId::partId` and shared across same-blueprint instances. A `null` cache builds uncached, which is how `player/Model.js` builds the player. Per-instance state (transform, material, worldAabb, detailedBounds, scatter, customTextures) is always fresh. Noise-pattern entity parts set `template.triplanar = true` and skip `faceTextureGroups` and UV scaling; `BuildObject` sets `material.textureScale` per mesh for them. Sphere UVs for other entity parts are pre-scaled by physical span (U → π·x, V → π·y·0.5).
  - **Bounds.** `computeBounds`, `computeWorldAabbFromGeometry` and `ComputeWorldAabbFromBounds` grow `UnitVector3` accumulators instanced once at entry, with the model matrix built once above the vertex loop. `computeObbFromMesh` and the collider shapes use the shared `math/Collision.js` helpers (`AabbCenter`, `AabbHalfExtents`, `SphereFromAabb`, `CapsuleFromAabb`).
  - Every mesh literal carrying a `displayTransform` populates `renderMatrixCache` via `math/Matrix.js`'s `CreateRenderMatrixCache` at construction, so `Render.js` needs no existence guard.
- `NewEntity.js` — Generic entity builder, shared by non-player entities and the player (see `player/Model.js`). Exports `NewInputFrame`, `BuildEntity`, `UpdateEntityModelFromTransform`, `ResetEntityToDefaultPose`, `SampleMovementPoint` and `ComposeTransform`. `BuildEntity(definition, surfaceMap, textureScale, faceTextureStore, geometryCache)` resolves `definition` through `NewTemplate.js`'s `ResolveEntitySource`, builds the model and collision, and returns `{ entity }`. `buildModel` builds its part index and parent→child links in two passes, so part order is unconstrained. `ComposeTransform` composes rotations through `math/Matrix.js`'s `ComposeEulerRotations`. The model's world position is the spawn surface's top-center (`getSurfaceOrigin`, stored as `model.surfacePosition`) plus `rootTransform.position`; after the first pose, the whole model is grounded so its AABB floor sits on that plane, then re-posed so `defaultPose` snapshots the grounded result. `computeDetailedBoundsForEntity` resolves the three collision layers from `collisionOverride`; `"auto"` physics resolves to `"capsule"` when the AABB is taller than wide/deep, else `"sphere"`. Sphere colliders are **inscribed** (`SphereFromAabb(aabb, true)`).

  **Re-posing.** `applyModelPose` records `model.posedTransform`. `UpdateEntityModelFromTransform` returns immediately when that matches `entity.transform` (`poseMatchesTransform`, exact equality); when only position moved it takes the translation path (`translateEntityDerivedState`); otherwise the full `refreshEntityDerivedState` recompose. Both end in `assignCollisionBounds`. Parts with `addsToBounds: false` (visual-only) get their world AABB from the 8 corners of their local `geometry.bounds` (`ComputeWorldAabbFromBounds`) and don't refresh `mesh.detailedBounds`; bounds parts keep the per-vertex sweep. `computeEntityAabb` and `placeAabbEnclosure` fold corners with `PointsAabb`.

  **The entity literal carries every runtime surface field.** `BuildEntity` declares `action`, `grounded`, `surfaceContact`, `surfaceNormal`, `alignedUp` and `referenceNormal` (the last three seeded to world up), the grounding and physics-hook fields (`buoyancyForce`, `launched`, `contactGrace`, `gripDemand`, `gravityScale` 1, `dragFree` `false`, `staticFriction` 0), `performance` (`collision` starts `false`, `animatedTexture` `true`), and the movement frame: `facing`, initialised from the authored rotation as `RotateByEuler(WORLD_NORMALS.Forward, rootTrans.rotation)`, and `inputFrame` from `NewInputFrame()`. That factory gives the carried heading (`carriedForward`, `previousCameraYaw`, `previousHasInput`, `previousGrounded`, `previousUp`), the control hand-off state (`previousKeys`, `previousControl`, `remapPending`, `holding`) and the loop lock (`locked`, `lockAngle`, `lockSign`, `lockThrottle`); `player/Master.js` reuses it on respawn. `modelTurnRate: { air, water }` is in radians/second from `defaultModelTurnRate` (8). All are declared here so `physics/`, `player/` and `handlers/game/Animation.js` read guaranteed fields.

  **Frozen rest colliders.** `captureRestCollision(definition, model)` poses the model at the origin with zero rotation and unit scale, measures once, and restores — producing `entity.collision.rest = { aabb, shape, physics, hurtbox, hitbox, groundCapsule }` in local space, at `BuildEntity` time only. `assignCollisionBounds` derives `aabb`, `simRadiusAabb`, `shape`, `physics`, `detailedBounds`, `hurtbox` and `hitbox` from that set plus `entity.transform`, so a collider is **placed**, never re-fitted. `placeBoundsForShape` mirrors `buildBoundsForShape`'s five cases: offsets pass through `transformOffset` (scale then rotation, via `rotateWithTrig`), `obb` axes rotate alone, radii scale by the transform's largest component, `aabb` becomes the world enclosure of the transformed local box, and `compound-sphere` alone still rebuilds from the posed model. `computeRotationTrig` computes the six trig values once per call. Face-to-world-axis alignment picks with `BestMatchOf`. `entity.transform` aliases `model.rootTransform` for position and rotation.
- `NewObstacle.js` — Obstacle builder. `BuildObstacle(source, index, options)` resolves `source` through `ResolveObjectSource(source, "obstacle")`, then builds the parts and a merged record carrying `worldAabb`; `options` carries `{ textureScale, faceTextureStore }`. `BuildObstacles` maps it over a list. Exports `MergeAabb` and `CreateDetailedBoundsFromParts`, which returns `null` for `collisionShape: "none"`, a triangle soup by default, and an envelope otherwise (a `"sphere"` obstacle gets `SphereFromAabb(bounds, false)`); `handlers/game/Simulator.js` reuses both after re-seating an obstacle.
- `NewTexture.js` — Procedural 2D texture generator and visual resource manager. Six patterns: `noise`, `tiles`, `stripes`, `grid`, `radial`, `linear`. `linear` is a vertical two-color gradient, structurally modeled on `radial`; it uses a 4-stop gradient (matching midtone colors at v=0/v=1, primary/secondary at the 0.1/0.9 margins) rather than a plain 2-stop ramp, because scatter batch geometry samples raw non-normalized local-space UVs under `GL_REPEAT` — a plain 2-stop gradient only ever samples its outer ~20% at each end with a hard seam at mid-height. Three element shapes for noise/tiles/grid cells: `circle`, `diamond`, square (default — note `drawShape` has no `"square"` case, so `null` and any unrecognized string both fall through to it). `ForEachTexturedMesh(sceneGraph, visit)` is the **single** enumeration of meshes that can contribute a texture id — terrain (`mode === "default"`), triggers, scatter meshes, obstacle parts (`mode === "default"`), entity model parts, and `waterVisual.body`/`waterVisual.top` — yielding each with a kind of `"terrain"` (spans its own texture), `"plain"` (carries no decals) or `"detailed"`. `collectTextureUsage` consumes it, and so does `handlers/game/Texture.js`'s `assignOwnerMeshes` (with entities excluded). The two walks were previously maintained by hand and had drifted — the missing `waterVisual` collection left `sea-surface` with no owner and froze the level loop on frame one of any level containing water — so a collection added here now reaches both by construction. `collectMesh` returns immediately for any mesh carrying `geometry.faceTextureGroups` (per-face meshes) — those never register a `material.textureID` usage entry, since `drawMeshList` binds per face group; registering one baked a dead canvas. `BuildFaceTextureData(store, ...)` returns the `faceTextureGroups` array itself.

  **Shape decals.** A shape decal's mask comes from `shapeMaskBuilders`, keyed by `shape`: `square`, `circle`, `triangle` and `pill` (a rectangle capped with a half-circle at each end). All four share `createMaskCanvas`. The mask bakes square and is stretched to the decal's authored size, so `compositeShapeDecal` passes the authored aspect (`localTransform.scale.x / .y`) and `pill` pre-divides its cap radii by it, keeping the caps round after the stretch. A new shape registers in three places: `shapeMaskBuilders`, `core/normalize.js`'s `shapeRequiredFields`, and `canonSchemas.json`'s `shape.allowedValues`. `compositeShapeDecal` resolves a decal's texture through the shared `decalTexture` schema (`texture.primary`/`texture.secondary`) and composites mask-last rather than via Porter-Duff `source-atop`, so the pattern surface's own per-pixel alpha survives and `secondary.a` is no longer structurally discarded. `detail` survives only as a deprecated alias of `texture`, `baseTextureID` as an alias of `texture.id`. `collectCustomTextures` walks a mesh/part's decal entries into a flat `customTextureUsage` list (image entries carry `opacity`, baked via `bakeImageDecalOpacity` when `< 1`). `ComputeCustomTextureSignature` derives a batch-identity signature from a part's decal array (used by `NewScatter.js`). `InitializeDecalDisplay(customTextures)` initializes the runtime transform/tint/swap display fields each decal needs. A decal opts into animation via an `animated` flag; `decalAnimationDefinition` attaches an animation definition only when the decal opts in *and* its preset's `animation.able` is true. `shape`, `compositeMode`, `holdTimeSpeed` and `blendTimeSpeed` are authorable on a preset entry and as a per-instance decal override; both the registry path and `ResolveTextureBlueprint` fall back to the preset value when the override is `null`.

  **Compositing.** Elements are painted through `fillReplacing`, which takes a `replace` flag from `resolveReplace`. That reads `compositeMode` — `"replace"`, `"overlay"`, or `null` meaning derive from `secondary.a < 1` — authorable on a preset and as a per-instance override (override wins when non-null), and a no-op for `radial`. Every engine-owned `texture.generated` literal carries the key explicitly. When set, it erases under `destination-out` before painting under `source-over`, so a translucent element **replaces** the pixel beneath it rather than blending. When clear (an opaque `secondary`), it paints once under plain `source-over`; running the erase anyway costs a correct antialiased rim. The erase/paint pair leaves a faint darker web at soft element boundaries, which is load-bearing for the `underwater` preset (whose `primary` and `secondary` are the same color). A translucent `secondary` over an opaque `primary` means that region is partly transparent. Noise coverage is `1 − exp(−density × shapeFactor)` (square 1, circle π/4, diamond 1/2): `speckSize` sets grain size only, so `density` is the sole control over the primary/secondary split. **Frequency patterns** (`tiles`, `stripes`, `grid`) carry their frequency in per-mesh UV tiling and bake only `speckSize`; **non-frequency patterns** (`noise`, `radial`) bake `density` into the canvas.

  **Face textures and ids.** Per-face face textures (noise only, sized to face UV span) are **content-addressed**: `buildFaceTextureSignature` keys on the base id, resolved density/speckSize/shape/colors, face pixel size and animation params, so identical content shares one canvas and one GL texture; `getOrBuildFaceTexture` dedups. `ResolveTextureBlueprint(textureBlueprint, textureDetail)` composes density/speckSize, applies the detail shape and colour overrides; the per-face path, `NewVoid.js` and `compositeShapeDecal` all use it. `ComputeGeneratedTextureID(generatedTexture)` derives the registry id, appending one segment per non-neutral override (`p=`/`s=`, `sh=`, `cm=`, `d=`, `ss=`). **Every field that changes the baked canvas must appear here**: the registry is shared by id and last-writer-wins. Preset colors are `{r,g,b,a}` objects everywhere; `rgbaToCss` converts for painting, `formatColorKey` renders a fixed-precision key segment. **A color object must never be interpolated into a signature string directly** — it stringifies to `[object Object]`. Each registry entry holds `{ id, definition, source, dirty }`; entries with `definition.animation.able === true` are picked up by `handlers/game/Texture.js`.

  **Resumable bakes.** `BeginTextureBake(definition, textureScale)` dispatches on `bakeKind` (`"face"` / `"surface"` / `"decal"`) to `BeginNoiseBake`, which paints the base fill and returns a job; `AdvanceNoiseBake(job, budget)` draws up to `budget` specks and reports completion. `handlers/game/Texture.js` is the sole caller and owns pacing. The `Math.min(16000, ...)` speck cap in `drawPattern`'s synchronous `noise` case is deliberately retained (its sizes are bounded at 512px); only the resumable per-face bake lifted its cap. `AddToVisualResources` registers textures for objects spawned mid-session. Exports `PrepareLevelVisualResources`, `AddToVisualResources`, `ForEachTexturedMesh`, `BeginTextureBake`, `AdvanceNoiseBake`, `BuildFaceTextureData`, `ResolveTextureBlueprint`, `BuildNoiseAnimationOptions`, `FREQUENCY_PATTERN_CONFIG`, `ComputeGeneratedTextureID`, `ComputeCustomTextureSignature`, `IsTextureTransparent`, `InitializeDecalDisplay`. It re-exports nothing: `NewObject.js` and `NewVoid.js` import `templates/textures.json` directly.
- `NewScatter.js` — Procedurally generated multi-part static scatter objects placed on terrain surfaces. Each object's open faces (see `NewVoid.js`) reach `iterateScatterInstances`, which rejects any sample whose XZ footprint AABB overlaps a top-relevant open face's XZ AABB, so scatter is not generated over openings. Only root parts are footprint-checked. `cloneScatterDecal` field-wise clones a part's decal array so batching never mutates the `textures.json` decal template. Batch identity is `${primitiveKey}_${textureID}_${customSignature}`; `primitiveGeometryKey` serializes `primitiveOptions` with `JSON.stringify`, so parts differing only in options get separate batches. A new batch clones its decals once and calls `InitializeDecalDisplay` on them. Placement matrices, the decal-bearing batch list and the ground-square grouping are built once in `Render.js`'s `buildScatterInstanceBuffers`.

  **Quality tiers.** `CONFIG.Performance.Scatter.Quality` selects what a blade is *made of*; it multiplies with `Scatter.Density`, which arrives as the `scatterMultiplier` parameter. **Any** field on a scatter template part may be tiered: an object keyed `low`/`medium`/`high` resolves by tier, anything else is a constant. All three keys are required — `resolveTier` is a total lookup (`value.low === undefined` is the discriminator). Resolution happens once per scatter type where `canonicalParts` is built, and clones after resolving. A part whose *resolved* primitive is `plane` is emitted **twice**, the twin yawed +90°, producing a fake-3D cross. That expansion happens where `partContexts` are built, **not** where `canonicalParts` is built: per-part yaw is `hashNoise(worldX, worldZ, seed + partIndex)`, so expanding earlier would give the twin its own yaw and the planes would stop being perpendicular. Both halves share `part` by reference, so the pair costs one extra instance, not a draw call. Authored today: `grass` tiers `primitive` (cylinder → cube → plane), `localRotation` and `localScale`; `pebbles` tiers `primitive` only; `bushes`, `leaves` and `branches` are untiered.
- `NewParticles.js` — Particle group generator **and** the per-group lifetime classes. Exports `GenerateParticles`, `ParticleGeneratorRequests` and `WithParticleOverride`. `GenerateParticles(request, textureScale, faceTextureStore, geometryCache)` takes `request = { templateId, position (UnitVector3 cnu), overrides, mode, target }` and returns `{ groups }`. It has no distance gate: generator requests build at any distance, and `handlers/game/Level.js`'s `SpawnParticles` drops a runtime burst beyond draw distance before calling it. The density multiplier `PERFORMANCE_SCALING.Density.Particles[CONFIG.Performance.Particles]` is read inline (`0`, the `Disabled` tier, warn-logs and blocks; otherwise it scales `count` via `Math.max(1, Math.round(...))`). Resolution (`resolveRequest`) uses null-sentinel precedence over six overridable keys — `count`, `spread`, `physics`, `color`, `velocity`, `duration`; `part` and `amount` are template-owned. A partial `overrides` object is valid and needs no padding. Group sizing is tiered by count: `< 3` gives N single-part groups, `3–7` three near-equal groups (`balancedSplit`), and `>= 8` samples a group count in `[max(3, ceil(N/maxGroupSize)), floor(N/minGroupSizeFor(N))]` and jitters the split within `[minGroupSizeFor(N), maxGroupSize]` — combinations are *sampled, never enumerated*. `maxGroupSize` is 10; `minGroupSizeFor(count)` returns 5 above 20, 3 above 10, else 2. `groupLayouts` holds 1–10-part offset tables as **dimensionless multipliers of the part's dimensions**: sizes 1–5 (`tunedLayouts`) are hand-tuned, 6–10 generated at import by `sphereLayout` (a golden-angle sphere). Each group's velocity is the template vector scaled 0.8–1.2× and perturbed inside a spread cone (`randomInCone`; 10°/30°/60° for `narrow`/`medium`/`wide`, 180° for `max`). Because that sample is drawn **once per group**, the number of distinct trajectories is the group count, not the particle count. Per-particle colour is written to `part.mesh.displayColor` by `writeParticleRgb(startColor, endColor, decayFactor, offset, target)`, never into `texture.generated.primary`; `offset` is one shared roll per part from `randomVariance`, so `variance` spreads along one hue and alpha is never randomized. `physicsFlagsFor(mode)` derives the five physics stage flags from `physicsMode` alone. The module-scoped `particleSurfaceMap` is a zero-origin surface, so the API takes no `surfaceMap`. `arcRate` and `shrinkFloor` are the two feel constants.

  **Lifetime classes.** `entity.particle` holds a `particleGroup` instance carrying an entity back-reference, its stored request, `duration`, `physicsMode`, cloned `spawnOrigin`/`spawnAxis`, `halfAngle`, `startColor`/`endColor`/`variance`, a per-part `offsets` array, `total`, `decayStart`, `finished`, `lifespan`, `targetId`. `endColor` is resolved once in the constructor (a clone of `startColor` when the authored `end` is `null`). `advance(deltaMs, deltaSeconds)` accumulates `total`, integrates motion for `"none"`, applies the arc for `"simple"`, and derives phase from `total` against `duration.holdTimeMs`/`endTimeMs` — frame-tracked, no wall clock. Alpha decay always resolves from the stored base (`displayColor.a = startColor.a * decay`, or `entity.transform.scale` for `"shrink"`), never multiplied. `expire()` fires at `total >= lifespan` and defaults to `recycle()`.

  Two subclasses extend the base directly, each overriding one member. `seedingParticleGroup` overrides `seedDue()` — only the first group of a `"generator"` request receives it; it emits `amount − 1` siblings at `intervalMs / amount` stride on a `seedClock` that `recycle()` doesn't reset. `burstParticleGroup` overrides `expire()` to set `finished = true` and narrows `lifespan` to `duration.endTimeMs`; `Level.js` splices finished groups out. The class is a three-way select on `request.mode` (`"generator" | "sibling" | "burst"`). `finished` and `lifespan` live on the base because the frame driver reads them on every group.
- `NewUI.js` — HTML UI element builder (`UIElement`). Produces DOM element trees consumed by `Render.js`. The HTML CONVERSION section exports `ParseHTML(html, options)`, which converts an authored HTML string into a **raw** menu payload — the shape a game hands to `ApplyMenuUI` — and returns it rather than applying it. `parseElement`/`parseChildNodes` walk the DOM; `readInlineStyles` reads inline `style` into `styles`; `collectStylesheet` gathers every `<style>` block verbatim into `menu.stylesheet`. CSS is never parsed, so rules apply as a real injected stylesheet and stay live against elements added later. `droppedTags` (`<script>`, `<link>`) are skipped. Conversion sits upstream of validation/normalization, so its output passes the same `normalizePayloadSchema` gate as hand-authored JSON.
- `NewBoss.js` — Boss entity builder. Specializes entity construction for boss characters.
- `NewTerrain.js` — Terrain builder. Exports `BuildTerrain` and `CarveOccluders`. `BuildTerrain(objects, world, faceTextureStore)` maps each definition through `ResolveObjectSource(definition, "terrain")`, then builds either one mesh (`buildSingleTerrain`, when there are no parts) or one independent mesh per part (`buildTerrainParts`, id `${objectId}:${partId}`, with root + local transform composition and per-part grounding). Returns `{ terrain, voidTerrain, meshes }` split by `mesh.meta.mode`, and logs the group count.

  **Occluders.** Every non-void terrain mesh gets an `occluder` record `{ inverse, pieces }` for `handlers/Render.js`'s occlusion culling. `buildOccluder` takes the mesh's `InscribedBox` — only for opaque, `mode: "default"` shapes that have one — subtracts each carving void's `VoidCutBoxes` (one box per tube stretch, along the void's `centerline`), and keeps the solid pieces from `SolidBoxes` (at least `Culling.MinBlockerSize` wide, largest 8). Each piece carries its local `min`/`max`, world `corners`, `worldAabb` and largest-face area. Records are built once from the build transform. `CarveOccluders(terrain, voidTerrain)`, called by `NewLevel.js` after `BuildVoidWalls`, rebuilds the records of every host a void cuts into, so the opening stays clear.
- `NewTemplate.js` — Shared template-reference and repeat-expansion resolver used by `NewTerrain.js`, `NewObstacle.js` and `NewEntity.js`. `clonePrimitiveOptions(part)` is a switch on `part.shape` with exactly one case: `tube` clones its root `thickness` and every node's four `Unit`/`UnitVector3` fields; everything else falls through to `structuredClone`. Exports `ResolveObjectSource(source, role)` (`role` `"terrain"` or `"obstacle"`) and `ResolveEntitySource(source)` (selects by the ref's `type`). Both are passthrough (same reference, no clone) when `source.shape !== "template"`. For a template reference (id existence guaranteed by `core/normalize.js`), it field-wise clones the boot-instanced template, expands any `repeat`-bearing part (`count` additional copies; copy *i* gets `localPosition += i·offset.position`, `localRotation += i·offset.rotation`, `localScale *= offset.scale^i`; ids `${partId}-r${i}`; `parentId` copied verbatim), then merges overrides at two levels — ref-level (`color`/`texture`/`scale`/`collisionShape`/`mode` for objects; `hp`/`collisionOverride`/`rootTransform` for entities) and per-part (keyed on the *authored* part id) — with null-sentinel precedence. Object refs resolve `dimensions`/`pivot`/`detail`/`destructible`/`hp`/`static`/`nullable` from the template's `shared` block and take `sticky` from the ref itself. Entity refs preserve `blueprintId`/`id` and take `type` from the template.
- `NewVoid.js` — Null-space void wall builder. `BuildVoidWalls(sceneGraph, textureScale)` attaches a per-pair `relations` map onto each null-space entry in place, physically carves the host geometry, and returns nothing. Called by `NewLevel.js` after terrain and obstacles are built. It imports no physics.

  **Cutting solids.** `buildCuttingSolid(triangles, aabb)` builds, for every host and void, each triangle's bounds and plane, the corner `vertices`, a `BuildTriangleGrid` grid, and a lazily filled `clearSides` array. Every containment and candidate query goes through the grid (`PointInsideTriangleGrid`, `TriangleGridCandidates`). `clearSideOf(solid, index)` caches which side of a wall the whole solid stays clear of; `outsideByWall` keeps a piece without further cuts when it stands off that side, and walls with a clear side are tried first when cutting.

  **Relations and openings.** Both drivers (`buildTerrainVoidWalls`, `buildObstacleVoidWalls`) cache each host's world triangles once (`buildWorldTriangles`) and seed a void's relations from `collectRelationHosts`: hosts start as `{ mesh, recordId, solid: null }`, and a host's cutting solid is built on its first strict AABB overlap with a void, followed by an actual `MeshesIntersect` test. Hosts declaring `nullable: false` are excluded. `buildVoidFaces` runs only for voids with at least one related host. Open faces come from `clipHostSurfaceToVoid`: the **host's own** surface clipped to the void solid — collect the void planes that cross the piece (`collectCrossingPlanes`), split with `SplitPolygonByPlane` (`splitPolygonBySolid`), keep pieces overlapping the void, drop slivers under `minimumOpenFaceArea`, and confirm each survivor by sampling `interiorSampleDepth` into the host (`pieceInsideSolid`). Survivors are instanced as world-space `UnitVector3` triangles. Each void carries `solidTriangles`, its world geometry, which `physics/Collision.js` uses for exact containment.

  **Carving works on polygons.** Host carving, lining clipping and open-face clipping work on convex corner lists, split into triangles only at output (`planeTolerance` 0.0001; `polygonArea` for sliver tests; boxes from `PointsAabb`). `buildVoidFaces` prepares each void triangle in local and world space with a cavity-facing normal whose sign comes from `resolveCavitySign` (sampled points tested against the void's own grid, majority wins), and sets `flipWinding` (`cavitySign < 0`) so the lining faces into the cavity. `buildHostLining(voidFaces, hostSolid, neighbours)` splits each face by every host plane that straddles it, keeps the pieces inside the host, and **subtracts every neighbouring void** carving the same host (`carvePolygonByVoid`), so overlapping voids produce one union boundary; lining output skips triangles under `minimumOpenFaceArea`. `appendLiningVertex` rebuilds local positions for crossings from barycentric weights. `buildVoidMesh` turns the lining into the renderable wall mesh (`occluded: false`, for occlusion culling), with `buildVoidCollision` splitting its world triangles into floor and non-floor one-sided `"voidWall"` soups.

  Pairing is **deferred**: `queuePairing` records the relation and queues the carve; `drainPairings` runs after both drivers, which is what makes the full neighbour set per host available.

  **Host carving.** Hosts are cut at build time. `carvePolygonByVoid` walks an explicit stack in which each piece re-selects its cutting planes from grid candidates; `carveHostGeometry` rewrites the host index buffer around its voids, reconstructing split vertices from `barycentricBasis`/`barycentricWeights`/`blendAxis`. Vertices are appended and never deleted, so bounds are unchanged — the pass writes only `geometry.positions`, `uvs`, `indices` and `faceTextureGroups`. `drainCarveQueue` runs at the end of `BuildVoidWalls`. Carving is render-only in effect: a host's collision box still spans the trench.

  **Logging** is two lines per build: `accumulateVoidTotals` folds each void's counts into one summary (ending with `ms=`), followed by a carve summary (hosts, triangle and vertex growth, worst host, elapsed ms).

  **Texturing.** The non-noise lining declares `geometry.triplanar` with `material.textureScale: 1`. The noise lining takes raw planar UVs from `GenerateFaceProjectedUvs` on the group's dominant axis, scaled by `textureScale / textureBlueprint.size`, and carries no `faceTextureGroups`, so it references the host's plain textureID as a tiling surface; `NewTexture.js`'s `collectTextureUsage` walks every lining so that id registers.
- `templates/` — JSON blueprint data, each collection keyed by template/blueprint id: `characters.json`, `enemies.json`, `projectiles.json`, `terrain.json`, `obstacles.json` (resolved via `NewTemplate.js`), `particles.json` (resolved via `NewParticles.js`), plus `levels.json` and `textures.json`. `player/characters.json` is canonicalized alongside these but backs player spawns, not `shape: "template"` refs. `core/normalize.js` may import from here (documented exception in MODULE_GROUPS.md). `Instance.js` exports `InstanceEngineTemplates()`, called once by `core/ini.js` at boot: it `structuredClone`s all eight engine-owned template singletons into a `raw` object *before* Unit-instancing them in place via `instanceCharacterTemplates`, `instanceScatterTemplates` (tier-aware: each tier of a `low`/`medium`/`high` map is instanced in place and the map kept), `instanceObjectTemplates`, `instanceEntityTemplates` and `instanceParticleTemplates`, sharing `canonicalizePartTransform` and `instanceModelPart`. `instanceModelPart` canonicalizes an absent `part.particle` to `null` and instances `tube` `primitiveOptions`. `raw` feeds `ENGINE.Blueprints.*` (authored shape); `instanced` is the same singletons mutated in place, read at call time by `player/Model.js`, `NewScatter.js` and `NewTemplate.js`. `loadTemplateDecalBitmaps` then starts an image load for every image decal across the templates. `templates/levels.json` holds the Simulator's level, whose camera is a custom set of `orbitCam` everywhere.

## Boundaries
**Called by:** `handlers/` (`Level.js`, `Simulator.js`, `Cutscene.js` and game-layer handlers); `physics/Master.js` and `handlers/game/Performance.js` import `UpdateEntityModelFromTransform`; `player/` imports `BuildEntity`/`NewInputFrame`. `NewParticles.js` is called by `NewLevel.js` at build time and by `handlers/game/Level.js` at runtime.
**Calls into:** `math/` (Matrix, Vector3, Utilities, Collision, Curves); `core/` (meta, config); `physics/Collision.js` (`NewLevel.js` only, `IsPointInSuppressingVoid` for surface chunks — a same-tier import). `NewTemplate.js` imports the five `templates/` collections directly; these are the boot-instanced singletons `Instance.js` mutates in place.
**Does not:** Manage persistent state, drive update loops, or write to the DOM or WebGL directly — all output is consumed downstream by `Render.js`.

## Invariants
- Builders are constructors: given scene input data, they produce scene output objects and hold no persistent state. Callers supply `faceTextureStore` and `geometryCache`; builders write into them, but ownership stays with the caller.
- The `sceneGraph` produced by `NewLevel.js` is the authoritative scene representation shared by the renderer and the physics pipeline.
- Builders attach two per-instance blocks at construction, fully populated and never lazily, so consumers read them unguarded: `renderMatrixCache` on every mesh carrying a `displayTransform`, and the `performance` flag block on every mesh `BuildObject` returns (void and invisible included), on obstacle records and on entities.
- The `renderMatrixCache` snapshot is **by value**, not by reference: `handlers/game/Animation.js`'s `writePartDisplay` mutates its display object in place, so a reference compare would go stale. In-place unit-mutating calls are confined to `core/normalize.js` and `builder/templates/Instance.js`.
- Non-template definitions are never touched by `NewTemplate.js`'s resolvers. `repeat` is a template-authoring-only field with no payload schema.
- Null-space data lives in a per-pair `relations` map on each null-space entry: `relations[relatedObjectId] = { suppressed, openFaces: Triangle[], voidWallMeshes: VoidWallMesh[] }`. `relatedObjectId` is a terrain mesh id or an obstacle *record* id; `relations` is always initialized.
- Build order in `NewLevel.js` is load-bearing: terrain and obstacles, then `BuildVoidWalls`, then `CarveOccluders` (which needs the void relations), then the `openFaces` lookup, and only then scatter batches, which reject samples over openings.
- `faceTextureStore` is a content-addressed plain object created in `NewLevel.js` and threaded through all builders, stored as `sceneGraph.pendingFaceTextures`. At runtime spawn, `handlers/game/Level.js` passes the live `textureRegistry` as the store.
- Level-authored particle generators resolve at build time. Any `levelPart`/`levelObject` (and template-ref equivalents) may carry `particle: { id, position, overrides }`; `NewObject.js` threads it into `mesh.meta.particle`, and `NewLevel.js` sweeps terrain meshes, obstacle records **and entities** — three `ParticleGeneratorRequests` calls materialized into one list *before* the generate loop, since that loop pushes groups into the same `entities` array. `mesh.meta.particle` is a guaranteed key (`null` or populated).
- `ParticleGeneratorRequests(carrier, kind)` is the sole authority on how a built carrier becomes generator requests, shared by the level build and `Simulator.Load`. Entity parts do carry generators; runtime-spawned entities are the remaining gap (see `DEFERRED.md`).
- Particle groups are ordinary entities in `sceneGraph.entities`. `entity.type === "particle"` is the runtime discriminator; `"particle"` is deliberately absent from `ENTITY_TYPES`.
- Particle geometry-cache keys are the bare `templateId` with part ids `p0..p9`, so a particle type costs at most 10 `partGeometryCache` entries. This holds only because per-particle size variation would go through `localScale`, never `dimensions`.
- `physicsMode` is the **sole authority** over a particle's five physics stage flags: `"none"` → all false and skipped from `ApplyPhysicsPipeline` by `Level.js`, the tick integrating position; `"simple"` → collision only, the tick applying the arc to `entity.velocity.y`; `"full"` → gravity + resistance + collision, the pipeline doing everything.
- The particle tick may write `entity.transform.position`, `entity.transform.scale`, `part.localTransform.scale` and `part.mesh.displayColor`. It must **never** write `part.mesh.transform.*`, `part.mesh.worldAabb`, any `entity.collision.*` field, or `mesh.displayTransform`; `applyModelPose` and `refreshEntityDerivedState` recompute those, and `syncEntityMeshes` already re-poses.
- Shrink scales `entity.transform.scale`, not per-part `localScale`, so each particle shrinks in place while the group's spread holds.
- `count` and `amount` are orthogonal. `count` is one emission, split across *in-phase* groups of up to 10 parts; `amount` is how many *phase-offset* emissions are alive at once. The performance tier scales `count` and leaves `amount` alone.
- Particle groups recycle rather than despawn: a level-authored generator's entity count is fixed at `groupCount(count) × amount`.
- `sceneGraph.partGeometryCache` is a level-scoped `Map` (keyed `blueprintId::partId`) of frozen part geometry templates, kept for runtime spawns. `Simulator.js` resets it on each `Load`.
- Consumers read the `relations` model directly (`physics/Collision.js`, `handlers/Render.js`, `NewLevel.js`'s `buildSurfaceChunks`, `NewTerrain.js`'s `CarveOccluders`). There is no stencil pass: the hole is real geometry, and `openFaces` survives for scatter suppression, the debug overlay and the build log.
- A `mode: "void"` object must be a closed solid; `core/normalize.js` drops one built from an open primitive (`plane`), and `NewVoid.js` carries no guard for it.
- A tube node's `localRotation` defines the **tangent at that node**, shared by the connector arriving into it and the one departing it. A connector is well-conditioned when both node tangents sit symmetrically about its chord; otherwise the centerline hooks. This is a property of the authored data.
- A tube node owns only the connector that **departs** it; `curved`/`smoothness` on the final node are never read.
- `buildTube` reuses `resolveCylinderSegments(complexity)` (8 / 12 / 16) for both ring resolution and connector centerline samples.
