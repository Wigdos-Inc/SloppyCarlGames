# math/ — Shared Math Layer

## Responsibility
Pure, stateless math utilities available to any engine module without restriction. Provides typed unit conversion primitives, 3D vector operations, matrix composition, physics force math, collision geometry helpers, and curve utilities. Together with `core/`, this is the engine's global helper layer — anything may import from it.

## Files
- `Utilities.js` — `Unit` and `UnitVector3` classes: typed value containers with conversion methods (`toRadians`, `toDegrees`, `toCNU`, `toWorldUnit`, `clone`) plus in-place component writers (`set`, `add`, `min`, `max` on `UnitVector3`). `CNU_SCALE` constant (fixed at development time). Conversion functions: `DegreesToRadians`, `RadiansToDegrees`, `CNUtoWorldUnit`, `WorldUnitToCNU`. Utility functions: `Clamp`, `Clamp01`, `Lerp`, `Squared`, `SmoothStep`, `ToNumber`.
- `Vector3.js` — 3D vector operations: `AddVector3`, `SubtractVector3`, `MultiplyVector3`, `DivideVector3`, `ScaleVector3`, `DotVector3`, `CrossVector3`, `LerpVector3`, `AbsoluteVector3`, `ClampVector3`, `CloneVector3`, `ToVector3`, `RotateByEuler`, `Vector3Distance`, `Vector3Length`, `Vector3Sq`, `ResolveVector3Axis`. Exports `WORLD_NORMALS` constant (canonical axis directions). `RotateTowardVector3(from, to, maxStep)` is the shared **unit-direction** rate limiter — a slerp step toward `to` capped at `maxStep` radians, returning `to` outright once within reach and picking an arbitrary perpendicular in the antiparallel case, where no shared plane exists. It is what every heading, reference-normal and model-pose ease in the engine turns on, so directions are limited on the sphere rather than by lerping components or interpolating Euler triples. Two shared predicates back caches in other groups: `Vector3Matches(a, b)` compares components directly (`a.x === b.x && …`) — an earlier `SubtractVector3(a, b) === ToVector3(0)` form allocated on both sides and could never return true — and `Vector3SqDistanceToAabb(point, aabb)` returns the squared point-to-box distance (0 inside), shared by `handlers/game/Texture.js` and `handlers/game/Performance.js`.
- `Matrix.js` — 4×4 matrix creation (`CreateIdentityMatrix`, `CreateModelMatrix`, `CreateRenderMatrix`) and transform composition helpers. `ComposeEulerRotations(parentRotation, localRotation)` multiplies both triples as `Ry·Rx·Rz` rotation matrices (rows built by the private `eulerRotationRows`) and decomposes the product back to Euler in the same order, taking roll on the axis at gimbal lock (pitch ±90°). The decomposition itself lives in the shared private `decomposeEulerRows(m, degenerateRoll)` — the inverse of `eulerRotationRows`, with `degenerateRoll` seeding the free angle at the ±90° pitch pole; `ComposeEulerRotations` passes `0`, having no prior roll to carry under a yaw-only parent. `EulerFromBasis(right, up, forward, degenerateRoll)` is the second entry point into it: the Euler triple for an orthonormal basis given as its three columns, used by `physics/Correction.js`'s `solveAlignmentRotation` and `handlers/game/Animation.js`'s `stepModelRotation` to convert a solved basis into a rotation without going through a matrix multiply. It is what `builder/NewEntity.js`'s `ComposeTransform` and `handlers/game/Animation.js` use to nest part rotations — adding the triples componentwise is only correct for single-axis chains. Exports `MultiplyMatrix4`, the shared 4×4 multiply used internally to compose translation/rotation/scale and by external callers (e.g. `handlers/Render.js`'s decal placement matrix) that need to compose matrices outside the transform-composition helpers. `CreateRenderMatrixCache(transform)` is a pure factory returning `{ matrix: Float32Array, position, pivot, rotation, scale }` — the render matrix plus a by-value snapshot of exactly the four transform fields `CreateRenderMatrix` reads. It sits next to `CreateRenderMatrix` on purpose: the snapshot's field set must stay in lockstep with what that function consumes, and colocating keeps the invariant in one file. Builders populate it on every mesh literal carrying a `displayTransform`; `handlers/Render.js`'s `renderMatrixFor` is the sole reader.
- `Collision.js` — Geometry math helpers: AABB overlap (`AabbOverlap`, `StrictAabbOverlap`), swept AABB/sphere tests, ray intersection against every bounds type, contact generation, and projection calculations. No state, no engine dependencies. `ClosestPointOnTriangle(point, a, b, c)` is exported for reuse by `physics/Collision.js`'s ground probe.

  **AABB vs triangle set.** `aabbTriangleSetContact` runs SAT of an AABB against the triangles overlapping it taken as **one hull**, smallest overlap winning rather than `chooseDeepestContact`'s deepest. Candidate axes are the three world normals plus each cluster triangle normal, deduped by `Math.abs(dot) > axisDuplicateDot` so anti-parallel faces collapse to one axis. Two exported wrappers select sidedness: `AabbTriangleSoupContact` (two-sided) and `AabbVoidWallContact` (one-sided, front faces only, rejecting a push no front face backs). They are registered as `aatr`/`traa`/`aavo`/`voaa` in `physics/Collision.js`'s `narrowphaseContact`.

  **Soup scans reject conservatively first.** The four soup contact loops (`SphereTriangleSoupContact`, `CapsuleTriangleSoupContact`, `SphereVoidWallContact`, `CapsuleVoidWallContact`) skip triangles outside a query box before running per-triangle contact math. The box is the query shape's own extent grown by its radius, built once per call by `sphereQueryBox`/`capsuleQueryBox` — both one-line adapters over a shared `segmentQueryBox(start, end, radius)`, the sphere passing its centre as both endpoints — and tested by `triangleInQueryBox`. It is the canonical `{ min, max }` AABB shape the rest of the file uses. `PointInsideMesh` likewise skips triangles lying wholly behind the ray origin on any axis, which `parityRayDirection` permits because all three of its components are positive. Both are pure rejects: an excluded triangle can neither produce a contact within the radius nor be crossed by the ray.

  **Void wall contacts respond on the geometric normal.** `sphereVoidWallTriangleContact` and `capsuleVoidWallTriangleContact` return the direction from the nearest point on the triangle to the nearest point on the query shape, not `triangle.normal` — matching what `capsuleTriangleContact` already does for ordinary soups, so a contact on a lining edge or vertex tilts rather than reporting the face plane. The one-sided gate above each return still tests `triangle.normal`, and the embedded case (no direction available) still returns it.

  **Solid queries.** A `SOLID QUERIES` section holds build-time predicates over closed triangle meshes, convexity-independent, used by `builder/NewVoid.js` to derive void openings: `TriangleAabb(triangle)`, `PointInsideMesh(point, triangles)` (ray-parity, cast along a deliberately off-axis `parityRayDirection` so an exact edge hit on axis-aligned geometry cannot break parity), `SplitTriangleByPlane(triangle, normal, offset)` (returns the pieces on both sides, fan-triangulated via the private `fanTriangulate`), and `MeshesIntersect(trianglesA, aabbA, trianglesB, aabbB)` (AABB reject, then the private `edgesCrossMesh` in both directions). These are the primitives that let a void's relation to a host be decided by an actual mesh-vs-mesh test rather than by face classification.

  **Ray-hit contract.** Every ray-hit result carries `{ hit, t, normal, inside }`. `inside` marks an **exit hit** — the ray began within the volume, so no surface faces the origin. `noRayHit` and `RayTriangleIntersect` always report `false` (a triangle has no interior); `RayAABBIntersect` sets it in its origin-inside branch (which returns the *exit* distance, not zero and not a miss); `RaySphereIntersect` sets it when the near root is behind the origin and the exit root is taken; `RayCapsuleIntersect`'s body roots set it on the same condition, its end caps inheriting from the sphere path; `RayOBBIntersect` propagates it. The field is present on every constructor rather than only where it can be true, so consumers read a guaranteed field. `limitRayHit` and `chooseClosestRayHit` pass whole result objects through, so it survives the triangle-soup and compound paths.
- `Forces.js` — Pure force math: `ComputeGravity`, `ComputeResistance`, `ComputeBuoyancy`, `ComputeStepVelocity`, `ComputeSubmergence`. Operates on numeric inputs only; no entity state or config access.
- `Curves.js` — Curve and frame utilities backing the node-chain `tube` primitive. `ApplyEasing(name, t)` resolves one of four named easings (`linear`, `easeIn`, `easeOut`, `easeInOut`). `SampleConnectorCenterline(startCenter, forward, endCenter, backward, smoothness, segments)` returns `segments + 1` centerline points `{x,y,z}` spanning one tube connector, with `points[0]` equal to `startCenter` and `points[segments]` equal to `endCenter`. It builds a **cubic Bezier with forward-aligned handles** — `p1 = startCenter + forward·d`, `p2 = endCenter + backward·d`, where `d = chord · 0.667` — so the curve departs along `forward` and arrives along `-backward`, pinning both end tangents for any corner angle including a 180° reversal. Each sample is `lerp(sharp, smooth, smoothness)`, blending the cubic against a single-center-corner hairpin through `cornerM = (p1 + p2) / 2`: `smoothness = 1` yields an even arc, `smoothness = 0` a hard corner at `t = 0.5`, and collinear input degenerates to a straight line. `ParallelTransportFrames(points, initialNormal)` returns a twist-free `{ tangent, normal, binormal }` per point via branch-free double reflection (Wang et al. 2008).

## Boundaries
**Called by:** Any engine module freely — no access restrictions.  
**Calls into:** `math/` modules may import each other (`Utilities.js` imports from `Vector3.js`).  
**Does not:** Hold mutable state, perform side effects, or import from handlers, physics, player, builder, or cutscene.

## Invariants
- All exports are pure functions or immutable constants, except `Unit`/`UnitVector3` which are value-holding class instances.
- `Unit` and `UnitVector3` are the canonical typed-value containers for all measurement values in the engine. They are instanced exactly once per value at a system entry point; downstream code operates on existing instances.
- `CNU_SCALE` is fixed at development time and does not change at runtime.
- `math/Collision.js` is geometry math only — it has no entity state, physics state, or config dependencies.
- `math/Forces.js` is pure math only — force assembly with entity/config context lives in `physics/Forces.js`.
- `RayAABBDetailedBoundsIntersect` passes the **caller's** `maxDistance` to the narrowphase, never `broadHit.t`. Detailed bounds sit inside the broad AABB, so every real narrowphase hit is at or beyond the AABB entry distance — capping at the entry `t` is structurally incapable of admitting a hit and can only reject one. It short-circuits and returns the broad hit for `detailedBounds.type === "aabb"`, and copies `broadHit.inside` onto the narrowphase result for `triangle-soup` (a soup cannot determine containment itself); `obb`/`sphere`/`capsule` compute `inside` on their own.
- `math/Curves.js` operates on plain `{x,y,z}` numeric vectors, not `Unit`/`UnitVector3`. Its tube callers extract centers and axes as raw numbers from composed frame matrices, so no instancing or de-instancing happens at this boundary.
- `SampleConnectorCenterline` needs no zero-length guard: the handle length multiplies the chord rather than dividing by it, so a zero chord degenerates to `p1 = startCenter`, `p2 = endCenter` without a divide-by-zero.
- The connector handle length is a single constant, symmetric fraction of the chord (`0.667`). It is not turn-angle aware, so asymmetric corners — where one node tangent lies near the chord and the other far off it — overshoot.
