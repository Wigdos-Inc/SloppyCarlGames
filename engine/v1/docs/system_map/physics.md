# physics/ — Physics Pipeline

## Responsibility
Computes and applies physics for moving entities each frame: gravity, buoyancy, fluid resistance, water currents, collision detection, and surface correction. The pipeline is externally driven — handlers pass entity state in and the pipeline mutates it in place. physics/ does not manage its own update loop.

## Files
- `Master.js` — Physics pipeline orchestrator. `ApplyPhysicsPipeline` is the external entry point called per physics-active entity per frame. Coordinates detection, force application, correction, and model sync internally. Also exports model-sync helpers used by `handlers/game/Level.js` for player and entity rebuild. `runPhysicsLoop` snapshots `frameStart` — `surfaceNormal` alone — before the correction loop and does **not** zero `transform.rotation.x`/`.z` at the top of the step, so the collider carries the previous frame's surface tilt through the whole step; `ApplyPlayerSurfaceOrientation` runs once at the end, after `ApplyGroundSnap` and the final overlap pass. `rotation.y` is never touched. It reuses the candidate array `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps` returns for the player's `ProbeGroundContact`, re-gathering via `BroadphaseCollectCandidates` only when that iteration's correction actually moved the entity (`overlapResolution.changedPosition`) — the broadphase ran before the correction, so only a move invalidates it.
- `Collision.js` — Broadphase and narrowphase collision detection against scene geometry. Trigger overlap detection. Scene spatial queries. Exports `GetSimDistanceValue`, which reads the active tier out of `PERFORMANCE_SCALING.SimDistance.Tiers` in `core/config.js` (Low 50 / Medium 100 / High 150 / Ultra 250 CNU) — the tiers are `Unit` instances declared there, and the accessor returns the shared instance rather than building one, so a per-entity per-frame gate costs a property read. `IsBeyondSimDistance(viewerPosition, targetPosition)` is the shared gate wrapping that threshold — a `null` viewer opts out entirely, since a payload may carry no player. `IsBeyondSimDistance` is now consumed once per tick by `handlers/game/Performance.js` (which resolves it into `entity.performance.physics` for every entity) and by `builder/NewParticles.js`'s generation gate; `Level.js`'s entity pass, `Collectible.js`'s pickup pass and `DetectCombatOverlaps` read the resolved boolean instead of recomputing. `DetectCombatOverlaps(playerState, entities)` correspondingly takes no `simRadius`/`cameraPos` arguments. Also exports `GetEntityPhysicsFlags(entity)`, which resolves `entity.character.physics` for the player and `entity.movement.physics` for everything else.

  Two scene queries are exported for use outside the pipeline. `BroadphaseCollectCandidates(sceneGraph, aabb, includeTriggers, includeEntities)` was promoted from module-private so `handlers/game/Camera.js` can broadphase its obstruction ray. `IsPointInSuppressingVoid(point, candidateId, voids)` (formerly the private `isGroundSupportInVoid`, body unchanged) answers whether a world point on a host lies inside a void that suppresses it — used by `ProbeGroundContact` for ground support and by the camera for obstruction.

  `ProbeGroundContact(entity, sceneGraph, groundSnapTolerance, candidates)` takes its candidate list as a parameter: its two full scans of `sceneGraph.terrain` and `sceneGraph.obstacles` collapsed into one loop over the already-filtered candidates, dropping the repeated `AabbOverlap`. `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps` retains and returns the array it built (`candidates`, an empty array on the collision-disabled early return) so the caller can hand it straight back.

  **Ground probe shape.** The probe point is `entity.collision.rest.groundCapsule`'s lower cap centre placed by the transform — scaled, rotated, translated, the same order `placeBoundsForShape` seats a narrowphase collider (see builder.md) — so grounding always uses the rest-pose capsule whatever collider the entity declares. `maxDist` is the scaled radius plus the snap tolerance. Three helpers take the probe point as a first parameter rather than closing over it: `tryAABB` (x/z containment, then vertical drop to `max.y`), `tryOBB` (closest point on the oriented box; a probe with no axis exceeded is *inside* the solid and rejected, and the contact normal runs from that closest point back to the probe rather than being a face normal — on a face interior the two agree, on an edge or corner the contact direction belongs to no face, with the largest-excess face normal as the zero-distance fallback), and `trySoup` (nearest up-facing triangle by true distance via `ClosestPointOnTriangle`, with `reachesTriangle` rejecting back faces and returning squared distance). Every accepted candidate is pushed to a `hits` array carrying `t`, `normal`, `triangle`, `type`, `surfaceId` and `bounds`; void-wall floors are probed the same way and appended under their host's source category. The list is sorted by vertical drop, and the **coupled** surface — `entity.physicsRuntime.groundSurfaceId`, the one currently grounding the entity — is taken in preference to the nearest, so another surface takes over only once the coupled one stops grounding. The return is `{ hit, normal, type, restDeltaY, surfaceId }`, where `restDeltaY = radius / normal.y - t` is the vertical move that seats the capsule on the winning surface. There is no support point: `ApplyGroundSnap` consumes `restDeltaY` directly.

  **Void suppression is per-contact.** `isVoidCancelled` cancels when the entity AABB centre is inside the void solid, or when the contact point stepped `contactMaterialProbe` (0.05 cnu) back along the contact normal into the host is inside it; a contact carrying no point falls back to the whole-body test. Both suppression tests attempt `NarrowphaseTest` against `detailedBounds` first and fall back to exact `PointInsideMesh` ray-parity containment against the void's `solidTriangles` — an additional accept path, so suppression can only widen. Both swept branches of `DetectPhysicsCollisions` evaluate it at the **end-of-frame** position (`vel`), not at the contact instant, where the body still sits outside the void. Void wall candidates carry a `sourceType` (`"terrain"`/`"obstacle"`) stamped by `BroadphaseCollectCandidates` and carried onto the pooled solid result, so `ResolveCollisions` resolves `sourceType || type` for its surface test and `buildGroundContact` reports the source category rather than the raw `voidWall` type.
- `Correction.js` — Post-collision position and orientation adjustment: surface alignment (`ApplySurfaceCorrection`), ground snapping (`ApplyGroundSnap`), player surface orientation (`ApplyPlayerSurfaceOrientation`). `CORRECTION_DISABLED` is the one frozen all-false result object, exported and reused by `Master.js`'s `noResult.correction`.

  `ApplySurfaceCorrection` measures its `MaxDeltaDegrees` continuity allowance against `frameStart.surfaceNormal` — the orientation the frame began with, which is world up while airborne — on **every** frame, not only frames that began grounded, so acquiring ground from the air takes the same allowance as walking onto it. A surface steeper than the allowance returns `resetSurfaceState`: unrotated, velocity unprojected. Velocity correction subtracts the into-surface component when it is negative and leaves the remainder at its own magnitude; it runs for every grounded surface, near-flat included, which is what replaced the old zero-out-on-landing branch.

  `computeAlignmentAngles(surfaceNormal, yaw)` rotates the normal into the entity's yawed frame before solving, returning `pitch = atan2(localZ, n.y)` and `roll = asin(-localX)` — matching `CreateModelMatrix`'s `Ry·Rx·Rz` composition, which a world-space solve inverts on both axes. `ApplyPlayerSurfaceOrientation` treats the ground capsule's cap centre as the pivot: it translates the entity by the difference between that anchor before and after the rotation, since rotating about the transform origin alone swings the cap off the surface the probe just accepted.
- `Forces.js` — Engine-shaped force assembly. Reads entity state and `CONFIG` to produce gravity, buoyancy, resistance, and submergence force vectors. Delegates pure math to `math/Forces.js`.
- `Current.js` — Movement velocity adjustments for entities inside underwater current volumes.

## Boundaries
**Called by:** `handlers/game/Level.js` — calls `ApplyPhysicsPipeline` directly from `Master.js` each frame. No intermediary Physics handler file exists in the current source. `Collision.js`'s scene queries are additionally imported by `handlers/game/Performance.js` (`GetSimDistanceValue`, `IsBeyondSimDistance`), `handlers/game/Camera.js` (`BroadphaseCollectCandidates`, `IsPointInSuppressingVoid`) and `builder/NewVoid.js` (`NarrowphaseTest`).  
**Calls into:** `math/` (Vector3, Utilities, Collision, Forces); `core/` (config, meta); `player/Master.js` (`TriggerPlayerRespawnSequence`) for player respawn; `builder/NewEntity.js` (`UpdateEntityModelFromTransform`) for post-physics model sync, used identically for the player and every non-player entity since the player build was unified onto the entity pipeline. The ground probe no longer derives its own capsule: it reads the frozen `entity.collision.rest.groundCapsule` and places it by the transform.  
**Does not:** Read user input, manage its own update loop, or drive rendering.

## Invariants
- `SimDistance` is the **single distance authority** in the engine. Every other reach is a fraction of it declared in `PERFORMANCE_SCALING.SimDistance.Fractions` (scatter cull radius, animated-texture bake cutoff, world-instance render cull, and fog saturation as a fraction of that cull), and the tier value itself gates enemy activation, collectible pickup, entity updates and particle spawn range. There is no second distance setting — a subsystem needing its own reach adds a fraction there rather than a config field, and raising a tier therefore moves gameplay range and visual range together.
- `ApplyPhysicsPipeline` is called once per frame per physics-active entity, in dependency order determined by `Level.js`.
- Displacement (`ScaleVector3(entity.velocity, deltaSeconds)`) is computed **outside** every physics-flag check, so zeroing an entity's stage flags does not make the pipeline inert — a non-zero `entity.velocity` still sweeps. Anything that owns an entity's motion itself must be skipped at the `Level.js` layer rather than relying on flags; particle `physicsMode "none"` is the current case.
- Particles are excluded from entity-vs-entity collision in **both directions**, and the two exclusions are separate. `BroadphaseCollectCandidates` skips `type === "particle"` when building candidates (a particle never obstructs anything), and its `includeEntities` parameter — which both call sites derive as `entity.type !== "particle"` — skips the entity collection entirely when the *querying* entity is a particle. Only the second stops a burst from destroying itself: a runtime burst spawns at its emitter's transform origin, so without it the emitter is a solid the particle is born inside, and `ResolveCollisions` strips the whole launch velocity along the contact normal on the first frame. Particles still collide with terrain, obstacles and void walls, which is correct — a ground normal opposes only downward motion and ejects an upward-moving particle out of the surface.
- Entity state is mutated in place by the pipeline; results are not returned as new objects.
- `math/Forces.js` contains pure numeric math; `physics/Forces.js` wraps it with entity and config context.
- `math/Collision.js` contains pure geometry math; `physics/Collision.js` contains detection logic with scene state.
- The player and non-player entities share one model-sync path (`UpdateEntityModelFromTransform`) and one collision-bounds shape (`aabb`/`physics`/`hurtbox`/`hitbox`); `Master.js` branches on `isPlayer` only for player-only stages (input-driven correction source, ground-contact probing, respawn, action-state events), not for model or collision-bounds representation.
- Colliders are **frozen at build time and only placed thereafter**. `entity.collision.rest` holds the local-space shapes captured from the rest pose (see builder.md's `captureRestCollision`); nothing in physics re-fits a collider from the posed model. `ProbeGroundContact` (`Collision.js`, player-only) reads `rest.groundCapsule` and places it by the transform; it neither re-derives a capsule nor reads a stored world one. It never gathers its own candidates either: the caller supplies them, and is responsible for handing over a set gathered against the entity's *current* position. The player's swept-solid detection path (`DetectPhysicsCollisions`) still differs from the non-player path (`DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps`) in how it gates candidates before the swept test — see `DEFERRED.md`.
- **The ground probe is the sole authority on grounding, and `physicsRuntime.groundSurfaceId` is its memory.** `runPhysicsLoop` writes the probe's `surfaceId` back after every probe, and the probe prefers that surface on the next frame. Hysteresis lives there and nowhere else — no other site decides which of several overlapping surfaces an entity is standing on.
- Penetration pushout resolves **along the detected contact**: `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps` takes `pushNormal`/`pushDepth` from the narrowphase contact produced by the entity's declared collider, never from a second axis-aligned narrowphase. The whole support-point chain that once backed this (`resolveBoundsSupportPoint`, `resolveContactSupportPoint`, `resolvePushSupportPoint`) is gone, along with `fillSolidResult`'s `candidateBounds` parameter — ground seating is `restDeltaY` from the probe.
