# physics/ — Physics Pipeline

## Responsibility
Computes physics for moving entities each frame: gravity, buoyancy, fluid resistance, water currents, collision detection, and surface correction. The pipeline is externally driven: handlers pass entity state in, and physics does not manage its own update loop. Detection, correction and force functions **return results**; `Master.js` is the only module that writes those results onto the entity.

## Files
- `Master.js` — Pipeline orchestrator. Its sole export is `ApplyPhysicsPipeline(entity, sceneGraph, deltaSeconds)`, called per physics-active entity per step by `handlers/game/Level.js`. It builds a per-call `physicsState` holding `displacement` and four stage blocks: `gravity`/`buoyancy`/`resistance` (each `enabled` from `CONFIG.Physics` and the entity's physics flags), `collision` (`enabled` is `entity.performance.collision !== false`; `triggers`, `hasUnresolvedPenetration`, `overlaps`), `correction` (`enabled`, `frameStart`, `groundReach`, `fold`, `groundContact`, `groundHits`) and `loop` (`iterations`, `hadMeaningfulWork`).

  **Force stages.** Submergence is computed whenever buoyancy or resistance runs, and it sets `underwater` at ≥ 0.5. Gravity (scaled by `entity.gravityScale`, which `player/Movement.js` lowers while coasting a loop lock), buoyancy and resistance (skipped while `entity.dragFree`) follow. For the player, floatiness then smooths the step's vertical change. A positive `entity.staticFriction` cancels the along-surface part of that vertical change (`physics/Forces.js`'s `GetStaticFriction`), so an idle player holds still on walkable slopes. Movement writes all three hooks every step. `displacement` is `velocity × dt`.

  **The collision skip.** `shouldSkipCollisionPipeline` skips `runPhysicsLoop` when the runtime cache is primed, no penetration is outstanding, displacement is under `EPSILON`, and position and rotation are unchanged since the last call (`physicsRuntime.previousPosition`/`previousRotation`).

  **`runPhysicsLoop`, in order:**
  1. `GetCorrectionFrame` freezes `frameStart` (`referenceNormal` and `physicsRuntime.groundSurfaceId`). It also sets `groundReach` (snap tolerance plus, with kept footing, |displacement| × sin(`MaxAngleDelta.Ground`)), the walkable `fold` (`{ normal: surfaceNormal, minCos }` with kept footing, else `null`) and this step's decayed `contactGrace`.
  2. Swept pass: `DetectPhysicsCollisions` → `ResolveCollisions(velocity, displacement, solids, fold)`, then a move. A new `collisionKey` fires `PLAYER_COLLISION`/`ENTITY_COLLISION` when the entity and `CONFIG.CustomEvents` both opt in.
  3. Up to three overlap/correction passes. Each one runs `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps` → `ResolveCollisions` with zero displacement, then `correctSurface`: `GatherGroundHits` → `PickGroundContact` → `GetSurfaceCorrection`, whose surface contact, grace, normals and velocity are written back. A probe hit writes `physicsRuntime.groundSurfaceId`, and a miss leaves it. The loop stops early when neither half changed anything.
  4. `grounded = ResolveGrounded(entity)`. `launched` clears once velocity along `alignedUp` is ≤ `EPSILON`. `gripDemand` and `referenceNormal` take `GetGripDemand`/`GetReferenceNormal`.
  5. `GetGroundSnap` seats the capsule along the contact normal.
  6. A final overlap detection stores the step's triggers. The player copies them into `playerState.activeTriggers`, which `camera/Master.js` reads for camera triggers.
  7. `GetSurfaceOrientation` makes the frame's single orientation write: rotation, then the position shift that keeps the pivot in place.

  **Detection reuse.** `moveEntity` applies every position change and clears `collision.overlaps` and `correction.groundHits`. The **player** reuses its last overlap detection and raw ground hits until a move clears them (`detectOverlaps`, `correctSurface`); other entities detect afresh each pass. Ground hits are re-classified and re-ranked every pass even when reused.

  After the loop, the death barrier clamps position and triggers `TriggerPlayerRespawnSequence` for the player; `PLAYER_GROUNDED_CHANGE` fires on a grounded flip when opted in.
- `Collision.js` — Detection against the candidates gathered for the entity this step, plus scene queries. Exports `BroadphaseCollectCandidates`, `IsPointInSuppressingVoid`, `HostVoids`, `DetectPhysicsCollisions`, `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps`, `DetectCombatOverlaps`, `ResolveCollisions`, `GatherGroundHits`, `PickGroundContact`, `GetEntityPhysicsFlags` and `NO_GROUND_CONTACT`.

  **Candidates come from the registry.** `BroadphaseCollectCandidates(sceneGraph, simRadiusAabb, includeTriggers, includeEntities)` lists terrain and obstacles (skipping `detailedBounds === null`, i.e. `collisionShape: "none"`), each void wall's `wallBounds` and non-empty `floorBounds` as separate `voidWall` candidates carrying a `sourceType`, triggers when asked, and other solid entities except the player and particles when asked. Entity candidates are `{ id, pairId, isTrigger, type, ref }`; their bounds are read live through `candidateAabb`/`candidateBounds`. `handlers/game/Performance.js`'s `UpdateCollisionCandidates` stores this list on `entity.performance.collision` once per step (see handlers.md), and every detection pass and the ground probe iterate it rather than broadphasing themselves. Camera rays and `builder/NewLevel.js` call the broadphase directly.

  **Swept detection.** `DetectPhysicsCollisions(entity, displacement, sceneGraph)` sweeps a sphere collider through `SweptSphereBounds`, anything else through `SweptAABB`. When a step is longer than the body's smallest AABB half-extent, it also traces the body's AABB center along the move against each triangle-soup candidate on the path box (front faces only, inside the path box). The nearest crossing that isn't void-cancelled becomes a solid with `tEntry = (t − reach) / moveLength`, so fast bodies cannot pass through soup. The player's contact is taken at the end of the move, else at the swept entry. Other entities reuse a cached contact per candidate (a `WeakMap` keyed by entity) until they move along its push axis. Solids are insertion-sorted by `tEntry`.

  **Overlaps.** `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps` gates each candidate on the tight `AabbOverlap` first, then takes the contact from `math/Collision.js`'s `NarrowphaseContact` against the entity's declared collider. Results go into shared pools (`fillSolidResult`: `tEntry`, `normal`, `pushDepth`, `type`, `sourceType`); overlaps push back by the contact's full depth.

  **Void suppression is per contact.** `isVoidCancelled` cancels a terrain/obstacle contact only where the material behind it was carved away: when the body's AABB center is inside a suppressing void's `solidTriangles` (`PointInsideMesh`), or when the contact point, stepped `contactMaterialProbe` (0.05 cnu) back into the host, is. A contact without a point falls back to a `NarrowphaseContact` against the void's bounds. Both swept branches test it at the **end-of-move** body. `IsPointInSuppressingVoid(point, candidateId, voids)` answers the same question for a single point; `HostVoids(sceneGraph, isTerrain)` picks the void list that carves terrain or obstacles.

  **Resolution.** `ResolveCollisions(velocity, displacement, solids, fold)` returns early with no hits. Otherwise it names a floor impact (highest `normal.y > 0.5`) and a wall impact (largest `HorizontalApproach` past `wallFacingMinApproachDot`), resolving a void wall under its `sourceType`. It slides through `math/Collision.js`'s `SlideAlongContacts`, which turns velocity across walkable folds instead of cutting it. It returns `collisionKey` (`"floor"`, `"wall"`, `"floor+wall"`, `"solid"` or `""`) and the changed flags.

  **Ground probe.** `GatherGroundHits(entity, sceneGraph, groundReach)` places the frozen `entity.collision.rest.groundCapsule`'s lower cap center by the transform (scale, then rotation, then translation). It collects raw hits within `radius + groundReach` from each terrain/obstacle candidate: `PointAabbFaceGap`/`PointObbFaceGap` ground only through a face the probe is over, and `NearestFrontTriangle` handles soup. It drops hits inside a suppressing void, and probes both lining soups of every void wall under the **void's** id and `sticky` flag. `PickGroundContact(entity, probe, frameStart)` classifies each hit with `Correction.js`'s `ClassifySurface` (speed ratio = tangent speed over `maxSpeed` for the player on a non-sticky surface, `Infinity` otherwise), drops walls, and ranks **walkable over sliding, then nearest `t`**, with the coupled `groundSurfaceId` breaking ties within `EPSILON`. It returns `{ hit, normal, contact, restDelta, surfaceId }` (`restDelta = radius − t`) or `NO_GROUND_CONTACT`.

  `DetectCombatOverlaps(playerState, entities)` tests hitboxes against hurtboxes for entities whose `performance.physics` is on, skipping collectibles and particles. `GetEntityPhysicsFlags(entity)` resolves `character.physics` for the player and `movement.physics` otherwise.
- `Correction.js` — Surface classification and correction results. Exports `ClassifySurface`, `GetCorrectionFrame`, `GetSurfaceCorrection`, `GetGroundSnap`, `GetSurfaceOrientation`, `GetGripDemand`, `GetReferenceNormal`, `PoseAnchor`, `ResolveGrounded` and `CORRECTION_DISABLED` (the frozen all-false result). None of them write the entity.

  **Surface classification.** `ClassifySurface(referenceNormal, candidate, currentContact, underwater, speedRatio, gripDemand)` returns `"walkable" | "sliding" | "wall"`, using the `Air` or `Water` half of `CONFIG.Physics.Correction.MaxAngleDelta`. Anything within `FlatSnapDegrees` of world up is walkable. A candidate no steeper than the reference is judged on absolute incline; a steeper one on the delta from the reference (walkable within the walkable limit, sliding within `Sliding`, wall beyond). The walkable limit is `Recover` rather than `Ground` mid-slide (hysteresis). Past the `Ground` incline, a would-be walkable contact slides when `speedRatio < gripDemand`.

  **`GetSurfaceCorrection(entity, groundContact, frameStart)`** returns the new `surfaceContact`, `contactGrace`, `surfaceNormal` (always the real normal), `alignedUp` (world up within `FlatSnapDegrees`, else the normal) and `velocity`. Velocity is **pinned** to the surface only with kept footing (grounded, or the same surface as `frameStart.surfaceId`), not `launched`, and grounded on the new contact. Pinning removes the whole normal component; otherwise only an into-surface component goes. Over a walkable crest, velocity is first turned onto the new facet with `TransportVector3`, and a walkable lift-off keeps its speed. A miss returns `releasedSurface`: contact `"none"` and, unless launched, a **contact grace** (`GROUNDING.ContactGraceMs`) during which `alignedUp` and `surfaceNormal` hold before returning to world up.

  **Snap, grip and reference.** `GetGroundSnap` applies only while grounded and within tolerance, offsetting by `restDelta` along the contact normal. `GetGripDemand` eases `gripDemand` toward `max(0, MinGripSpeed × (cos(Ground) − normal.y) / (cos(Ground) + 1))` at `MinGripSpeed / GROUNDING.GripRampMs`, holding while airborne. `GetReferenceNormal` decays toward world up at `ReferenceReleaseRate` once contact-less past the grace, adopts `surfaceNormal` outright on a walkable contact that moved, and otherwise holds.

  **Orientation.** `GetSurfaceOrientation(entity)` returns nothing during the contact grace. Otherwise it solves the exact standing rotation with `math/Collision.js`'s `SurfaceAlignedRotation(alignedUp, facing, rotation, onBack)` (a full basis through `EulerFromBasis`; `onBack` for a sliding contact). It also returns the position offset that keeps `PoseAnchor(entity)` fixed: the scaled rest ground-capsule cap while touching a surface, the rest-AABB center when releasing one. `handlers/game/Animation.js` turns the eased model pose about the same anchor. `ResolveGrounded(entity)` is a pure derivation: walkable contact, minus a launch veto measured along `alignedUp`, minus buoyancy exceeding gravity.
- `Forces.js` — Engine-shaped force assembly over `math/Forces.js`: `GetGravity` (applies `gravityScale`), `GetBuoyancy`, `GetResistance`, `GetSubmergence`, `GetFloatiness` (the character's air/water floatiness) and `GetStaticFriction`. Each returns a value; `Master.js` writes it.
- `Current.js` — Movement velocity adjustments for entities inside underwater current volumes (currently a two-line stub).

## Boundaries
**Called by:** `handlers/game/Level.js` calls `ApplyPhysicsPipeline` directly; there is no intermediary physics handler. `Collision.js` is also imported by `handlers/game/Performance.js` (`BroadphaseCollectCandidates`, `GetEntityPhysicsFlags`), `handlers/game/Enemy.js` (combat), `camera/Master.js` (`BroadphaseCollectCandidates`, `IsPointInSuppressingVoid`, `HostVoids`) and `builder/NewLevel.js` (`IsPointInSuppressingVoid`). `Correction.js`'s `PoseAnchor` is imported by `handlers/game/Animation.js`.
**Calls into:** `math/` (Vector3, Utilities, Collision, Matrix, Forces); `core/` (config, meta); `player/Master.js` (`TriggerPlayerRespawnSequence`); `builder/NewEntity.js` (`UpdateEntityModelFromTransform`, used identically for the player and every other entity).
**Does not:** Read user input, manage its own update loop, drive rendering, or broadphase during detection (the registry supplies candidates).

## Invariants
- `SimDistance` is the **single distance authority**. Every other reach is a fraction of it in `PERFORMANCE_SCALING.SimDistance.Fractions`, and consumers read `PERFORMANCE_SCALING.SimDistance.Tiers[CONFIG.Performance.SimDistance]` directly; there are no accessor functions.
- `Master.js` is the only physics module that writes entity state. Detection, correction and force functions return results, and their frozen "nothing" results (`NO_GROUND_CONTACT`, `CORRECTION_DISABLED`, `noDetection`) are shared, never mutated.
- Collision runs only when `entity.performance.collision` is a non-empty candidate list. `UpdateCollisionCandidates` sets it every step before the player pipeline: always for the player, otherwise only when `performance.physics`, `CONFIG.Physics.Collision.Enabled` and the entity's collision flag are all on.
- Displacement is computed **outside** every physics-flag check, so zeroing an entity's stage flags does not make the pipeline inert. Anything that owns its own motion must be skipped at the `Level.js` layer; particle `physicsMode "none"` is the current case.
- Particles are excluded from entity-vs-entity collision in **both directions**: `BroadphaseCollectCandidates` never lists a particle, and a particle's own registry pass passes `includeEntities = false`. Particles still collide with terrain, obstacles and void walls.
- Colliders are **frozen at build time and only placed thereafter**. `entity.collision.rest` holds the local-space shapes (see builder.md's `captureRestCollision`); the ground probe reads `rest.groundCapsule` and places it by the transform.
- **The ground probe is the sole authority on surface contact, and `physicsRuntime.groundSurfaceId` is its memory.** It is written after every hit, never on a miss; `PickGroundContact` uses it to break ties and `GetSurfaceCorrection` reads it through `frameStart` for kept footing. `camera/` reads it for ramps and loop surfaces.
- **Physics never imports camera.** Camera-driven behaviour reaches physics only through fields Movement writes (`gravityScale`, `dragFree`, `staticFriction`).
- **`grounded` is derived, never assigned.** `ResolveGrounded` produces it and `runPhysicsLoop` applies it once per step. A consumer that wants to know *what* the entity stands on reads `surfaceContact`.
- **`referenceNormal` is the last walked surface, written once per step.** `frameStart` freezes it for the loop. Adoption is instant and release is eased, on purpose.
- **`physics/` writes exact orientation only.** `GetSurfaceOrientation` is not rate-limited, because the collider, probe and pivot read that rotation the same step. Visual smoothing is `handlers/game/Animation.js`'s model-pose channel, which never writes back.
- Penetration pushout resolves **along the detected contact** from the declared collider's narrowphase, never a second axis-aligned test. Ground seating is `restDelta` from the probe.
- The player's swept path (`DetectPhysicsCollisions`) still has no tight `AabbOverlap` gate on solids, unlike `DetectCurrentPhysicsOverlaps` — see `DEFERRED.md`.
